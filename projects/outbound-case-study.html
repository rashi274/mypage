AI-Powered Outbound & Sourcing Engine

Status: Live / Production-Ready Stack: Apollo, Clay, HubSpot, OpenAI (GPT-4o Mini)

1. Executive Summary

Cold email campaigns typically suffer from low conversion and high bounce rates due to generic copywriting, poor list curation, and outdated email validation.

This project implements an **AI-driven outbound prospecting pipeline** that automates sourcing from Apollo, firmographic scraping in Clay, and hyper-personalized message generation using GPT-4o Mini. Piped directly into HubSpot, the engine successfully verified 47 out of 49 contacts and automatically staged personalized follow-ups.

2. Outbound Architecture

Below is the data flowchart representing the prospecting funnel. Targets sourced in Apollo are enriched and waterfall-verified in Clay, passed through an AI copywriting module, and synced directly to CRM outreach segments.

3. Technical Breakdown

Step 1: Company Sourcing (Apollo API)

Sourced 27 target companies matching our Ideal Customer Profile (ICP): early-stage B2B SaaS companies in India with fewer than 50 employees.

Step 2: Clay Waterfall Enrichment & Scraper

We load the company list into Clay.com. Clay executes a waterfall verification query to extract key metadata: 

{
  "company_domain": "stripe.com",
  "employee_count": 8300,
  "industry": "Fintech / Payment Processing",
  "description": "Stripe builds economic infrastructure for the internet."
}

Step 3: Finding & Verifying Executive Contacts

Clay automatically crawls the company domain to find the email addresses of Founders, CEOs, or GTM Heads. The found emails are routed through a verification API (Debounce/Hunter) to block invalid addresses, resulting in a **96% deliverable email verification hit rate** (47 out of 49 contacts).

Step 4: AI Copywriting & Hyper-Personalization

To bypass spam filters and increase response rates, we pass the company bio and title details to GPT-4o Mini to write a tailored opening line.

System Prompt: Write a 1-sentence opening line for a cold email based on this company bio: "{{company_bio}}". Focus on what they build. Do not use generic greetings like "Hope this finds you well". Keep it under 15 words.

// Example AI-generated hook for a Stripe prospect:
"Saw how you're simplifying payment infrastructure for global startups—neat platform."

Step 5: CRM Integration & Pipeline Tracking

Once enriched and validated, the 47 contacts are pushed to HubSpot, placed inside a custom segment labeled "Project 1 - Outreach Ready", and assigned to a 5-stage sales tracking pipeline: 

# Syncing lead card with personalized AI variables to HubSpot CRM
def upload_outbound_lead(email, name, company, personal_opener):
    url = "https://api.hubapi.com/crm/v3/objects/contacts"
    headers = {"Authorization": "Bearer HUBSPOT_KEY", "Content-Type": "application/json"}
    body = {
        "properties": {
            "email": email,
            "firstname": name,
            "company": company,
            "custom_email_opener": personal_opener, # Injected in email sequence
            "lead_status": "READY_TO_OUTREACH",
            "pipeline_stage": "PROSPECT"
        }
    }
    requests.post(url, json=body, headers=headers)
